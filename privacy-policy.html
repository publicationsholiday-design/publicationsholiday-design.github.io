Privacy Policy

Rhyme Puzzle Studio · Effective date: 4 November 2026

This privacy policy applies to the following apps and games published by Rhyme Puzzle Studio on Google Play and the Apple App Store, each called "the app" below:

  • Word Tap – Search Letters (also listed as Word Tap)

Developer: Rhyme Puzzle Studio

This policy explains what information our apps use, why, and the choices you have. "We", "us" and "our" mean Rhyme Puzzle Studio, the developer of the apps listed above.

Summary

  • You do not need an account. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or other personal details.
  • Your progress and settings are stored only on your device.
  • Apps that show ads use Google AdMob. Google's ad software collects some device data, as described below.
  • We do not run servers that collect your data, and we do not receive, sell or rent your personal information.

1. Information stored on your device

Our apps save your progress and settings locally on your device so you can continue where you left off. Examples include levels or words solved, scores, streaks, rewards, and preferences such as sound and reminders.

This data is not sent to us. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data in your device settings.

2. Advertising (apps that show ads)

Apps that show ads, including Word Tap – Search Letters, use Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. The Google Mobile Ads SDK built into these apps collects the following data automatically:

DataWhat it is
IP addressYour device's IP address, which may be used to estimate its general location (not precise GPS location).
Device and advertising identifiersOn Android: the advertising ID and app set ID. On iOS: the advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking.
Product interactionsFor example app launches, taps, and ad and video views.
Diagnostic informationPerformance information about the app and the ads SDK.

Google uses this data to show ads, measure ad performance, provide analytics and prevent fraud. Data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit.

Google's own policies explain how it uses this data:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

Your choices:

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: the app asks for your consent using Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time with Privacy settings in the app.
  • iPhone and iPad: the app asks for permission to track through Apple's App Tracking Transparency. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", your advertising identifier is not used. You can change this in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Google or Privacy > Ads settings.

Ads may be personalised or non-personalised depending on your choices and your location.

3. In-app purchases (apps that offer them)

Purchases are processed by Google Play or Apple. We do not receive your payment card or billing details, only confirmation that a purchase was made so the app can unlock it.

4. Reminder notifications (apps that offer them)

If you turn on reminders, they are scheduled locally on your device; no server is involved. You can turn them off in the app or in your device's notification settings.

5. Sharing

If you tap Share, the app opens your device's share menu with a short text, such as your result. It is sent only to the app or person you choose. We do not receive it.

6. Ratings

Our apps may show the standard Google Play or App Store rating prompt. Ratings and reviews are handled by Google or Apple under their own privacy policies.

7. Permissions

Our apps request only the permissions their features need. Word Tap – Search Letters does not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location.

  • It uses internet access for ads and the advertising ID permission.
  • If reminders are turned on: notifications, plus permission to restore scheduled reminders after your phone restarts.

8. Children

Our apps are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through one of our apps, please contact us and we will help remove it.

9. Data retention and deletion

  • We do not keep any of your personal information on our own servers.
  • App data on your device is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.
  • For data collected by Google for advertising, use the advertising ID or tracking controls described in section 2, or contact Google through its privacy policy.
  • If you have any request about your data, email us and we will respond.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example when we add an app to the list above. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
