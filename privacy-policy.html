This policy explains what information our games use, why, and the choices you have. "We", "us" and "our" mean Rhyme Puzzle Studio (Tejinder Singh), the developer of the games listed above.

Summary

  • You do not need an account. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or other personal details.
  • Your progress and settings are stored only on your device.
  • Games that show ads use Google AdMob. Google's ad software collects some device data, as described below.
  • We do not run servers that collect your data, and we do not receive, sell or rent your personal information.

1. Information stored on your device

Our games save your progress and settings locally on your device so you can continue where you left off. Examples include levels or puzzles solved, scores, streaks, hints or rewards, and preferences such as sound and reminders.

This data is not sent to us. It is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data in your device settings.

2. Advertising (games that show ads)

Games that show ads use Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. The Google Mobile Ads SDK built into these games collects the following data automatically:

DataWhat it is
IP addressYour device's IP address, which may be used to estimate its general location (not precise GPS location).
Device and advertising identifiersOn Android: the advertising ID and app set ID. On iOS: the advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking.
Product interactionsFor example app launches, taps, and ad and video views.
Diagnostic informationPerformance information about the game and the ads SDK.

Google uses this data to show ads, measure ad performance, provide analytics and prevent fraud. Data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit.

Google's own policies explain how it uses this data:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

Your choices:

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: the game asks for your consent using Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. Where the game offers Privacy settings, you can change your choice there at any time.
  • iPhone and iPad: the game asks for permission to track through Apple's App Tracking Transparency. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", your advertising identifier is not used. You can change this in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Google or Privacy > Ads settings.

Ads may be personalised or non-personalised depending on your choices and your location.

3. In-app purchases (games that offer them)

Purchases are processed by Google Play or Apple. We do not receive your payment card or billing details, only confirmation that a purchase was made so the game can unlock it.

4. Reminder notifications (games that offer them)

If you turn on reminders, they are scheduled locally on your device; no server is involved. You can turn them off in the game or in your device's notification settings.

5. Sharing

Some games let you share a result. If you tap Share, the game opens your device's share menu with a short text. It is sent only to the app or person you choose. We do not receive it.

6. Ratings

Our games may show the standard Google Play or App Store rating prompt. Ratings and reviews are handled by Google or Apple under their own privacy policies.

7. Permissions

Our games request only the permissions their features need, such as internet access for ads and, on Android, the advertising ID permission. Games with reminders also use notifications, plus permission to restore scheduled reminders after your phone restarts.

Our games do not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location.

8. Children

Our games are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through one of our games, please contact us and we will help remove it.

9. Data retention and deletion

  • We do not keep any of your personal information on our own servers.
  • Game data on your device is removed when you uninstall the game or clear its data.
  • For data collected by Google for advertising, use the advertising ID or tracking controls described in section 2, or contact Google through its privacy policy.
  • If you have any request about your data, email us and we will respond.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example when we add a game to the list above. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
